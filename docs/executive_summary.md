# Executive summary

## Decision

MercatoVivo should begin with Lentini store operations, introduce margin gates for promotions, and review pricing and product mix in Pantry and Meat & Seafood. These actions address the clearest combination of target underperformance, margin pressure, waste, and availability risk in the supplied 2025 extract.

## Findings

### 1. Lentini is the first operational priority

MercatoVivo Lentini Etna finished 6.22% below its annual sales target, the largest shortfall among the five stores. Its gross margin was 45.65%, below the 46.39% chain result. It also recorded the highest waste rate at 4.03% and all 296 stockout days in the inventory extract.

This is not a simple overstock problem. Waste and stockouts appear together, so a blanket inventory reduction could make availability worse.

### 2. Promotions protect activity but dilute margin

Promotion lines produced €4,912.70 of net sales at a 35.76% gross margin. Non-promotion lines produced a 46.86% gross margin. The 11.10 percentage-point gap supports tighter promotion economics, but it does not prove that promotions caused incremental sales.

The lowest promotion margins were Ferragosto Sicilia at 29.12%, Weekend Mozzarella at 29.76%, and Natale in Dispensa at 30.81%.

### 3. Margin pressure is concentrated by category

Pantry generated the most category sales at €18,965.88 but delivered a 41.86% margin. Meat & Seafood generated €17,951.53 at a 42.16% margin. Both are below the chain result. By contrast, Private Label products delivered a 49.30% margin compared with 44.38% for National brands.

The mix result supports a targeted review, not an automatic assortment replacement. Volume, customer response, and substitution risk still need to be tested.

## Recommended actions

| Owner | Action | Expected effect | Monitor monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store Operations Manager | Run a 6-week Lentini replenishment review for Dairy, Fresh Produce, and Meat & Seafood. Set product-level minimum/maximum stock and investigate each stockout day. | Reduce waste without increasing lost availability. | Waste rate, waste cost, stockout days, sales variance |
| Commercial Director | Require a pre-launch gross-margin floor and post-campaign review for every promotion. Test a holdout or matched comparison where practical. | Protect gross profit and identify promotions worth repeating. | Promo margin, units, sales, average discount, measured uplift when available |
| Category Managers | Review cost, price, and pack architecture in Pantry and Meat & Seafood. Test selective Private Label visibility where a comparable alternative exists. | Recover margin while protecting units and basket value. | Category margin, net units, price index, Private Label share |

## Limitations

- The data are synthetic and represent a teaching extract.
- Promotion comparisons are descriptive rather than causal.
- Inventory stockout days are concentrated by design and should be interpreted as an exception signal.
- The static dashboard preview is generated from validated output tables. A Power BI `.pbix` file is not included.
