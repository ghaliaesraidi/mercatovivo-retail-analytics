## What this means for the portfolio

The project should not be presented as “I made a dashboard.” The stronger statement is:

> I audited multiple retail data sources, defined commercial KPIs, built a reliable analytical model, explained store/product profitability and target variance, and translated the findings into prioritized management actions.
