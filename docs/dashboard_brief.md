# Power BI Dashboard Brief

## Page 1 — Executive overview

**Question:** Where is management attention required now?

- KPI cards: net sales, gross profit, gross margin %, sales vs target %, transactions, waste rate
- Monthly sales and gross-margin trend versus target
- Store performance matrix: sales variance versus margin variance
- Three dynamic callouts for the most important exceptions
- Slicers: date, store, category

## Page 2 — Store and category performance

**Question:** Which combinations drive or destroy profitable growth?

- Store/category matrix with net sales, gross profit, margin %, and target variance
- Top/bottom product ranking
- Private-label versus national/local brand comparison
- Monthly small multiples by store
- Drill-through to product detail

## Page 3 — Promotions and customers

**Question:** Which commercial activities deserve repetition or review?

- Promotion sales, promo margin %, units, and average discount
- Promo versus non-promo margin comparison
- Campaign scatter: sales versus gross margin, sized by units
- Loyalty segment: basket value, transactions, and units per basket
- Clear note: descriptive comparison is not causal uplift

## Page 4 — Waste and availability

**Question:** Where can waste be reduced without creating stockouts?

- Waste units and estimated waste cost
- Stockout days versus waste-rate quadrant
- Store/category heat map
- Weekly trend for perishable categories
- Exception table with store, product, waste, stockouts, and suggested owner

## Visual rules

- One screen per business question.
- Use euros for money, percentage formats for rates, and consistent decimal precision.
- Prefer direct labels and informative titles over legends.
- Use red only for confirmed adverse variance; do not decorate with many colors.
- Keep definitions available through tooltips.
- Every headline KPI must reconcile to a saved SQL result.
