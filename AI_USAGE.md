# AI Use Disclosure

AI was used as a learning assistant and project-production aid for:

- researching recurring requirements in Italian analyst vacancies;
- designing the fictional business case and synthetic-data generator;
- suggesting documentation structure, audit tests, SQL exercises, and dashboard requirements;
- reviewing clarity, consistency, and reproducibility.

The portfolio owner is responsible for:

- completing and explaining the data audit;
- deciding and documenting cleaning rules;
- writing, running, and validating analytical SQL;
- building and reconciling the Power BI model;
- interpreting results and choosing recommendations;
- verifying every statement presented as a finding.

No real customer or company data are used. Final conclusions must not be copied from AI output without independent validation against the project data.
